# Embryo Change Watch

Embryo Change Watch is Embryo-Zero's fork of [changedetection.io](https://github.com/dgtlmoon/changedetection.io) (fdce084, commit fdce084). It does watches pages for changes.

- Our changes live on the `embryo` branch. The original license files (LICENSE, NOTICE) are unchanged.
- Legal notes: LEGAL.md. Dated list of our changes: CHANGES-EMBRYO.md.
